About my approach

Bringing three fields
into conversation.

My doctoral research in applied linguistics examined language learning, educational mobility, and identity. At Iowa, I am extending this preparation through special education and applied behavior analysis, with particular attention to communication intervention and caregiver participation.

My current research examines caregiver learning, the use of intervention in family life, and observed child communication as related but distinct concerns. Across research, teaching, and practice, I ask what support people need to learn, whether it is workable in their everyday activities, and what the evidence shows has changed.
