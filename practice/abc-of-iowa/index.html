My previous clinical role

At American Behavioral Consulting of Iowa, I implemented ABA interventions supporting autistic children’s learning and social skills. I also participated in caregiver coaching under the supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst.

This experience informs the questions I bring to my research: what support people need to learn a procedure, how it fits everyday routines, and how to understand the evidence of change.

American Behavioral Consulting of Iowa is an ABA provider in the Iowa City–Cedar Rapids region. It is included in the University of Iowa Center for Disabilities and Development’s provider directory and the Iowa Association for Behavior Analysis’s organizational listings.

These public resources offer a way to learn about the organization within Iowa’s wider network of disability and behavior-analytic services.
