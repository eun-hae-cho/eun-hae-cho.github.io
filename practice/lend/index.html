The program

Many perspectives.
A shared commitment.

Iowa LEND prepares graduate students, family members, self-advocates, and professionals for leadership in disability services. Its curriculum connects person- and family-centered care with interdisciplinary collaboration, research, and advocacy.

Program overview · The areas below describe Iowa LEND’s curriculum and opportunities.

01

Learning across disciplines

The curriculum brings healthcare, education, and community perspectives into dialogue through seminars, interdisciplinary clinic observations, and faculty mentoring.

02

Families as teachers

The Parents as Mentors experience centers families’ knowledge of everyday life. Family members and self-advocates also contribute to the program’s teaching and leadership community.

03

Leadership through inquiry

Research projects or literature reviews, individual learning plans, and disability policy activities connect professional preparation with questions about services and participation.
