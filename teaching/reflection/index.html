Continuing development

Reflecting on my own teaching.

I use students’ questions, their work, and feedback from supervision to reconsider my explanations, pacing, and expectations. Field supervision has also taught me the value of frequent check-ins instead of waiting until formal observations to identify a need for support.

As I work toward the University of Iowa Certificate in College Teaching, I am using this portfolio to connect my teaching decisions with evidence of learning. My expanded review of caregiver learning also prompts a question I bring to teaching: what can a learner do with support, what can they use independently, and what makes that learning workable in practice?

A continuing commitment

Patience means maintaining clear standards while giving learners time, feedback, and repeated opportunities to improve.
