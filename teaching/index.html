My approach

High expectations, explicit support, and room to improve.

I use modeling, guided practice, feedback, reflection, and revision to make expectations visible. My experience spans elementary English, university writing, graduate co-teaching, special education, and teacher-candidate supervision.

My teaching philosophy explains the theoretical foundations of this approach; the experience pages show where I have practiced it. Assessment and reflective practice connect those experiences with my continuing graduate learning.
