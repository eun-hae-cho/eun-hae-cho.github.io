Special education & field supervision

EDTL 4900 · Fall 2025

Foundations of Special Education

In a graduate teaching practicum supervised by Jillian Thoele, I led three weeks of instruction within a 15-week undergraduate course. I also supported the course as a teaching assistant during the second half of Spring 2025.

My instruction asked students to examine common disability myths and consider how assumptions shape access, interpretations of evidence, and instructional decisions.

University Field Supervisor · Spring 2026

Supporting teacher candidates

I supervised four teacher candidates across four Iowa City Community School District placements, working with cooperating teachers and completing four observations per candidate.

My responsibilities included proposal, midterm, and final meetings, written observation notes, and feedback on developing classroom practice.
