Academic communication & language teacher education

Graduate InstructorDepartment of Applied Linguistics2019–2024

In Composition for American Academic Communication II (ESL 015), I taught international undergraduates academic writing through exemplars, guided practice, feedback, and revision. This experience made the need for explicit expectations central to my teaching.

I co-taught graduate courses in Pragmatics in Language Learning and Teaching (APLNG 585, Fall 2023) and Narrative Research in Applied Linguistics (APLNG 597, Spring 2024) with Celeste Kinginger. I also taught American Oral English for International Teaching Assistants III (ESL 118G, Summer 2024).

Across these settings, I learned to connect disciplinary concepts with the communicative tasks and professional situations learners encounter.
